Where it works

3 places business owners can see impact quickly.

Sales

Lead list preparation, outreach drafts, follow-up reminders, call summaries, and sales leakage alerts.

🚀 Upside: faster response, cleaner follow-up, better pipeline visibility, and fewer missed revenue opportunities.

Operations

Turn messy emails, WhatsApp threads, PDFs, and spreadsheets into action lists and next steps.

⚙️ Day-to-day: summarize updates, assign tasks, prepare checklists, chase next steps, and reduce repeated admin work.

Finance

Overdue account tracking, polite reminder drafts, risk grouping, and collection status summaries.

📂 Back-office: payment reminders, invoice checking, document preparation, reporting support, and routine finance follow-through.
