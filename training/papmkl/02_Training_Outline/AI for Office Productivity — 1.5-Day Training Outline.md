

| Course Title:  AI for Office Productivity: Work Smarter with ChatGPT, Gemini Notebook & Personal AI Agents |
| :---- |
| **Overview:**  This 1.5-day interactive training programme helps office professionals use generative AI to work more efficiently and confidently. Through demonstrations, guided exercises and workplace scenarios, participants will learn to improve prompts, personalise ChatGPT, use Gemini Notebook for research and document-based work, and apply AI to everyday tasks such as drafting emails, summarising reports, preparing meeting materials, organising information and developing action plans. The programme concludes with a hands-on workshop in which participants create, test and refine a focused personal AI agent for a recurring office task.  |
| **Target Participants:**  Executives, managers, team leaders, administrative and operations personnel, and other office-based professionals who want to improve workplace productivity with AI. No programming or technical background is required.  |
| **Learning Outcome:**  By the end of the session, the participants will be able to: Identify practical opportunities, limitations and responsible-use considerations for AI in everyday office work. Write and refine effective prompts by providing clear context, instructions, constraints and output requirements. Personalise ChatGPT for individual roles, communication preferences and recurring workplace tasks. Use AI to draft, rewrite, summarise and structure emails, reports, meeting notes and work plans. Use Gemini Notebook to research, analyse and synthesise information from workplace documents. Explain the fundamentals of AI automation and agents, including appropriate safeguards and human review. Create, test and improve a focused personal AI agent for a recurring office productivity task. |
| **Duration: 1.5 days (in-person)**  Schedule: Day 1, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; Day 2, 9:00 AM–1:00 PM  |
