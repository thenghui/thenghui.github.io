Section D · Verification Method

How This Verification Was Done

1Live link checking

Every URL in this sheet was requested programmatically on 29 September 2026 and only links returning a successful response were included. Numerous plausible-looking links found during research returned “404 – not found” and were rejected.

2YouTube title & channel verification

Each video was checked against YouTube itself — both its title and publishing channel were confirmed, so no dead or misattributed video links are listed.

3Known caveats

  • Tableau’s website blocks automated checkers; its pages were confirmed by direct content retrieval and open normally in a browser.
  • “Gemini Notebook” is Google’s current name for the tool previously called NotebookLM — tutorials titled “NotebookLM” teach the same product.
  • Microsoft Support links use the /en-us/ paths; equivalent localised paths exist for other locales.

4What needs no external source

Four items are purely internal and carry no factual claims: the welcome session, the ice-breaker, the competency check and the Workplace Improvement Project. All remaining curriculum items map to at least one official step-by-step lesson.
