Programme Overview

What This Programme Delivers

A four-hour, hands-on virtual programme that turns everyday AI use into a repeatable working method. Participants practise on realistic sample data with free or personal accounts: ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for writing, research, summarising and analysis, and Google Antigravity for agentic building and code.

The programme also covers careful review of AI-assisted code, and non-text workflows for images, meeting audio, documents and screenshots. Every module follows the same five-step habit — Brief → Context → Draft → Verify → Ship — so the method survives the tool.

Participants leave with a tested brief, a working prototype or a credible one-page spec, a reviewed bug fix, and a 30-day plan with one task, one tool and one proof point.

AI Team Members Software Engineers Product Managers
Prerequisites & preparation. No prior AI or prompt-writing experience is required. Bring a laptop, a browser and free personal accounts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google Antigravity. Engineers should be comfortable running commands in a terminal for the coding lab. All exercises use fictional sample data; no confidential company data is uploaded to free tiers.

Programme Deliverables

Delivery Note

The programme runs live online with three breakout labs, so participants practise rather than watch. Demonstrations use realistic fictional data — support tickets, a small buggy repository, screenshots and a meeting transcript — and the same sample material is used in the participant workbook. Free or personal tool tiers are sufficient; paid plans are optional and confirmed in advance. Sessions can be recorded with participant consent, and every generated image, transcript or prototype is labelled as AI-assisted.
