# Training Outlines

Practical, hands-on AI training programmes by [Ts. Chong Theng Hui](03_Trainer_Profile/CTH-HRDC-Trainer-Profile.html) —
delivered in-house, face-to-face, and HRDC-claimable. Each outline includes learning outcomes, a
module-by-module breakdown, activities, and training hours.

All programmes are hands-on and can be tailored to your team's tools, workflows, and industry.

## AI Tools for Beginners

Entry-level programmes for everyday users. No technical or design background required.

### ChatGPT for Beginners

*Practical AI Skills for Everyday Work*

A beginner-friendly, hands-on 1-day programme that equips everyday users to use ChatGPT confidently
at work — from first conversations and effective prompts to practical everyday tasks, responsible
use, and a personal action plan.

**1 Day · 7 Training Hours · 6 Modules** · ChatGPT (OpenAI)
