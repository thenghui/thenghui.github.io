Programme summary

Course Title:AI-Powered Property Management: Build Your Smart Virtual OM, BM & BE Assistant
Overview:A practical workshop for the LDS property management team to prepare a reusable Virtual Property Management Assistant in ChatGPT. Participants set its role, tone, instructions and reference documents, then practise complaint handling, meeting minutes, notices, quotation comparison, preliminary house-rule checks and security onboarding. Each participant leaves with a property-specific prompt toolkit and demonstrates one reviewed workflow using approved sample documents.
Target Participants:LDS Operations Managers (OM), Building Managers (BM) and Building Executives (BE). Beginner to intermediate level; basic computer literacy required. No programming knowledge required.
Learning Outcome:By the end of the programme, participants will be able to:
  • Identify suitable property management tasks for generative AI and explain its limitations.
  • Prepare reusable assistant instructions and prompts using approved house rules and SOPs.
  • Draft complaint acknowledgements, progress updates and closure messages, meeting minutes and resident notices.
  • Build a quotation comparison that records scope, costs, warranties, exclusions and questions requiring specialist review.
  • Locate potentially relevant clauses in supplied house rules and strata references, and verify each reference against the original document.
  • Produce a security onboarding briefing and an operational reporting template from approved material.
  • Test an AI-assisted workflow, correct unsupported output and prepare a personal adoption action plan.
Duration:1 day · 7 instructional hours (420 minutes), excluding lunch and refreshment breaks. Instructor-led workshop with demonstrations, case studies and hands-on practice. Date, venue and daily start/end times: TBD.

Training methodology

Suggested distribution across the seven instructional hours.

20% · Concepts

AI fundamentals, responsible use and prompting principles.

30% · Demonstrations

Step-by-step walkthroughs of property management workflows.

50% · Hands-on practice

Guided exercises with property documents, testing and refinement.

Pre-workshop preparation

  • Bring a laptop, internet access and access to the organisation-approved AI platform. The trainer should confirm account access, permitted document handling and the exercise workflow before the workshop.
  • Prepare your property house rules in soft copy, together with relevant SOPs. Use PDF, Word or Excel files as appropriate; retain readable document titles and page or clause identifiers.
  • Prepare sample complaints, meeting notes or minutes, supplier quotations, resident notices and operational reports. Remove personal, sensitive and confidential information, and obtain approval for all material used with the AI platform.
  • The trainer should provide or confirm the applicable versions of the Strata Management Act 2013 (Act 757) and Strata Management (Maintenance and Management) Regulations 2015 reference documents before training.
  • Use trainer-provided sanitised examples if property documents cannot be shared. Saved instructions and prompt templates form the reusable assistant; the workshop does not depend on a particular paid assistant-building feature.

Assessment and trainer feedback

  • Assistant setup: Instructions define the role, tone, output format, approved sources and how to handle uncertainty; a test question shows the instructions being used.
  • Practical work: Participants complete complaint, minutes, notice, quotation, reference-check and onboarding activities; outputs are checked against the supplied facts and documents.
  • Final demonstration: Each participant demonstrates one workflow, explains corrections made after checking it and identifies where human approval is required.
  • Action plan: The participant records a first workplace use case, the person who will review it and the next implementation step.

Participant deliverables

  • Property-specific Virtual OM/BM/BE assistant instructions and reusable prompt toolkit.
  • Complaint acknowledgement, progress update and closure drafts; meeting minutes and notice templates.
  • Quotation comparison template with gaps, assumptions and specialist clarification questions.
  • House-rule reference checklist with verifiable document, page or clause references.
  • Security onboarding briefing, duty checklist and assessment questions, plus an operational report template.
  • Personal AI adoption action plan and one demonstrated workflow.

Review before operational use

AI output is a draft for review. Check statements, calculations and references against the supplied originals. Legal interpretations, procurement recommendations and technical evaluations require verification by qualified personnel before management decisions. A reference checklist supports preliminary review and does not establish compliance.
