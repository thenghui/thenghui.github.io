AI for Office Productivity
Work Smarter with ChatGPT, Gemini Notebook & Personal AI Agents

An interactive, hands-on programme that equips office professionals to use generative AI confidently — from better prompts to building your own personal AI agent.

1.5 Days · 11 Training Hours Day 1: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM Day 2: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM In-Person · Hands-On No Technical Background Required
Programme Overview

What This Programme Delivers

This 1.5-day interactive training programme helps office professionals use generative AI to work more efficiently and confidently. Through demonstrations, guided exercises and workplace scenarios, participants will learn to improve prompts, personalise ChatGPT, use Gemini Notebook for research and document-based work, and apply AI to everyday tasks such as drafting emails, summarising reports, preparing meeting materials, organising information and developing action plans.

The programme concludes with a hands-on workshop in which participants create, test and refine a focused personal AI agent for a recurring office task.

ExecutivesManagersTeam LeadersAdministrative PersonnelOperations PersonnelOffice-Based Professionals
No programming or technical background required.  All exercises use everyday AI tools on a laptop or mobile device.

Learning Outcomes

By the End of the Session, Participants Will Be Able To:

01

Identify practical opportunities, limitations and responsible-use considerations for AI in everyday office work.

02

Write and refine effective prompts by providing clear context, instructions, constraints and output requirements.

03

Personalise ChatGPT for individual roles, communication preferences and recurring workplace tasks.

04

Use AI to draft, rewrite, summarise and structure emails, reports, meeting notes and work plans.

05

Use Gemini Notebook to research, analyse and synthesise information from workplace documents.

06

Explain the fundamentals of AI automation and agents, including appropriate safeguards and human review.

07

Create, test and improve a focused personal AI agent for a recurring office productivity task.
